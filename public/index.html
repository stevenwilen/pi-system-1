Shown at the top of your day as reference, so you can plan around what is already happening. Nothing on it is ever changed.

  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer. The phone app cannot do this.
  2. Click the gear, then Settings.
  3. In the left column, under Settings for my calendars, click the calendar you want.
  4. Scroll down to Integrate calendar.
  5. Copy the Secret address in iCal format.

That address lets anyone who has it read your whole calendar, so treat it like a password. It is stored against your account and never shown back to you here.
